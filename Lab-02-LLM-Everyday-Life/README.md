# Lab Sheet 02 — AI in Everyday Challenges
  
**Group:** 08 — The Budget-Challenged Student  
**Product:** Pocket Path

## Objective

Use a large language model (LLM) as a product-development partner to design a solution to an everyday student problem. Group 08 focused on small purchases such as food, transport, coffee and printing, which can add up before students realise where their monthly allowance has gone.

## Tasks and Implementation

We designed **Pocket Path**, a mobile budgeting app concept for first-year university students living away from home on a fixed monthly allowance. The target user is new to budgeting, pays with both cash and card, and often runs short in the last week of the month.

### How we used Claude

1. **Interpret the brief.** Claude helped break the challenge into three goals: understand, control and manage spending.
2. **Develop the concept.** Claude suggested a product name, target user and features. Our group chose which suggestions to keep.
3. **Design the mock-up.** Claude drafted a build prompt and helped design the app screens and their workflow.
4. **Prepare the pitch.** Claude drafted the slides. Our group edited them and wrote the script for the 2–3 minute presentation.

### App design

| Goal | Feature | How it works in the proposed app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand | Quick Add | Enter an amount and tap a category to log a cash or card purchase. The design targets a logging time under five seconds. |
| Understand | Spending Insights | Show totals by category so students can see how repeated purchases, such as coffee, add up. |
| Control | Safe to Spend Today | Divide the remaining monthly allowance by the number of days left and recalculate after each expense. |
| Control | Category Budgets | Set monthly category limits and show a warning when spending approaches a limit. |
| Manage | Subscription Tracker | List recurring payments with their next billing dates and total monthly cost. |

The mock-up covers five screens: Home, Quick Add, Insights, Budgets and Subscriptions. A student logs an expense in Quick Add, and the proposed app updates the daily amount on Home, the category totals in Insights and any relevant budget warning. The design stores expenses, budgets, subscriptions and allowance settings on the phone and supports manual entry without a login or bank connection.


## Observations

- The design challenge was making small expenses easy enough to record consistently. The Quick Add mock-up addresses this with two inputs: an amount and a category.
- The daily amount is easy to understand, but it depends on students keeping their expense records current. An upcoming subscription can make the estimate too high if the student has yet to account for it.
- The concept PDF uses both “Trickle” and “Pocket Path.” This README uses **Pocket Path**, the name used in the group presentation. The mock-ups show the intended workflow, and the five-second entry time is a design target.

## Learning Outcomes

- Use an LLM to interpret a brief, brainstorm product ideas and draft prototype and presentation materials.
- Translate a user problem into specific features and a screen-by-screen workflow.
- Review and edit AI-generated material for consistency, and distinguish design targets from measured results.
